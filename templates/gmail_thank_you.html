Thank you for your fireball sighting report 


This may be the only email you will get but we are grateful and appreciate
your report. 

The data you have entered is added to the database at https://www.imo.net/ 
and an email is sent from there to key people at Fireballs Aotearoa. Usually
this is the first notice we get that a fireball has occurred and we can then 
check our network of cameras. 

For this, accurate timing and the direction of the sighting is extremely 
useful.

Keep in mind that a fireball begins at about 100kms above the earth's 
surface, so, if you see a fireball near the horizon, it is probably much 
further away than it appears

Thank you once again for your report and for your support of 
Fireballs Aotearoa and the Global Meteor Network.
